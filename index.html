Common Help Topics

Getting Started

Add your battery setup, devices and charging sources. PowerCalc compares estimated daily use with estimated generation.

Battery Capacity

Enter battery capacity, usable percentage, state of charge and inverter efficiency to estimate available watt-hours.

Loads & Devices

Add each device with its wattage and estimated daily runtime to calculate estimated daily energy use.

Solar & Charging

Add solar panels or other charging sources with output, charging hours and efficiency to estimate daily generation.

Trip Planning

Create a trip scenario to estimate how your battery setup may perform over multiple days.

PowerCalc Pro

Pro includes saved trip scenarios, export tools, advanced planning features, presets and additional battery-bank options.

Purchases & Subscriptions

Restore Purchases

If PowerCalc Pro is not showing after purchase, use Restore Purchases in the app while signed into the same Apple ID used for the purchase.

Manage or Cancel

Subscriptions purchased through Apple are managed through your Apple ID subscription settings.

Troubleshooting

  • Close and reopen PowerCalc.
  • Check your battery, wattage, runtime, charging-hour and efficiency values.
  • If Pro features are missing, try Restore Purchases.
  • If the issue continues, email support with what happened.

Important

PowerCalc is a planning tool. Results are estimates based on the values you enter and are not live battery telemetry. Actual results can vary because of battery condition, temperature, charging losses, weather and real-world device use.
