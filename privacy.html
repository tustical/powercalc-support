Privacy Policy

PowerCalc

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how PowerCalc handles information when you use the app.

Information You Enter

PowerCalc lets you enter information such as battery capacity, device power use, charging sources, and trip-planning details so the app can calculate estimated energy use and battery performance.

This information is used to provide the app's planning and calculation features.

Purchases and Subscriptions

PowerCalc Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment information and transaction processing. PowerCalc does not receive your full payment card information.

Support

If you contact PowerCalc support, we may receive the information you choose to include in your email, such as your email address, device details, screenshots, and a description of the issue.

Support information is used only to respond to your request and troubleshoot problems.

Sharing of Information

PowerCalc does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers only when needed to operate the app, process App Store purchases, provide hosting, or respond to support requests.

Data Retention

Information you send to support may be retained as long as reasonably necessary to resolve your request, maintain support records, or meet legal obligations.

Children's Privacy

PowerCalc is not specifically directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the app.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if PowerCalc's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
